**1. MAIN PURPOSE**

We're looking for a creative and curious Communications Assistant to join our team. The role will play a hands-on role in developing engaging content across platforms including social media, print and digital.

The main purpose of this role is to help shape the council's approach to engaging a younger audience through creating content.

Working to briefs, you'll help plan, research and produce written, visual and audio materials that support wider media, marketing and documentation campaigns. This is the ideal role for someone who is passionate about storytelling, enjoys working with a range of media formats, and wants to learn how to connect messages with audiences in meaningful and measurable ways.

You'll collaborate with colleagues across communications and contribute to the full content lifecycle: from planning and creation to storage, distribution and evaluation.

The post holder will work closely with the council's youth participation team, collaborating to understand Merton's young people and create content that resonates with them.

The post holder will complete the Content Creation Apprenticeship Level 3 run by provider JGA. Through this programme they will develop the knowledge, skills and behaviours (KSBs) that underpin effective content creation and communications practice, including audience understanding, content production, data-informed decision making, campaign planning and brand application.

**2. MAIN DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

**Content creation and writing**

1.  Create engaging multimedia content for the council's social media channels, contributing ideas to increase followers and reach, with a particular focus on helping the council reach more young people.

2.  Monitor and respond to queries on social media accounts as needed, adapting content to respond to evolving circumstances.

3.  Adapt communications content for different channels and platforms, including website, social media, e-newsletters and the council magazine, understanding how audience behaviour and communication needs differ across each channel.

4.  Contribute ideas, research, source images and write for the council's e-newsletters and printed resident magazine.

5.  Ensure all written and visual content adheres to the council's brand guidelines.

6.  Research and write news stories about council policies, services and campaigns, identifying key messages and applying the appropriate tone of voice, style and inclusive language for a diverse range of target audiences and channels.

7.  Explore emerging social media trends and channels and research whether they are right for the council and make recommendations to the communications team and senior leaders.

**Campaigns and planning**

1.  Under the guidance of the Senior Communications Officer, develop, plan and implement proactive content within communications campaigns that promote the council's policies and services.

2.  Update the communications grid and calendar, planning content delivery against the schedule and operating effectively within the production workflow to meet deadlines.

3.  Put together evaluation reports showing how communications activity has helped the council meet its objectives, using data to assess success and identify areas for improvement for future campaigns.

4.  Work with the youth participation team to gain insights from young people in Merton and create content help them engage with council communications.

**Media relations and stakeholder engagement**

1.  Work with Communications Officers to provide guidance on communications messaging and channels to councillors and officers to support and promote their work, including delivering tailored training sessions.

2.  Develop and maintain effective working relationships with colleagues, officers, elected members, journalists and suppliers.

3.  Work with Communications Officers to provide a responsive and efficient service for journalists.

**Compliance and administration**

1.  Ensure all work takes account of legal requirements, including media law, copyright, intellectual property rights, GDPR, web accessibility, data protection, rules governing local government publicity, and the council's policies and protocols.

2.  Undertake any other relevant tasks which may be assigned such as administrative duties, including some finance processes, monitoring shared inboxes, diary management and bookings for team meetings.

3.  Work out of hours from time to time, including evenings and weekends, at corporate events, council meetings or other communications-related activities.

This is a politically restricted post.
